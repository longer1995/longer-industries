## 1. Why Twitter/X

- **Defense procurement officers, contractors, and journalists** hang out daily

- **No gatekeepers** — a first-generation ammo manufacturer can be in the same thread as a retired 4-star general
- Elon's algorithm **rewards long-form threads and engagement**, perfect for expertise-based content

Brandon's unfair advantage: He's not an analyst or think-tank talker. He's **done the deals** — manufactured ammo, navigated international arms regulations, raised capital, and operated in the messy reality of defense commerce. That perspective is rare and valuable.

## 5. Growth Tactics

### Month 1-3: Foundation (0 → 2,000 followers)

1. **Follow and engage 50 accounts/day** in defense, industrial, startup, and Houston business spaces
2. **Reply to every large account** in your niche with substantive, expert replies (not "great take!")
3. **Tag relevant people** in threads (defense journalists, industry accounts)
4. **Cross-post to LinkedIn** (repurpose threads as LinkedIn articles for dual exposure)
5. **Join Twitter Spaces** as a speaker — defense/geopolitics rooms are active and welcoming to real operators

### Month 3-6: Acceleration (2,000 → 10,000 followers)

1. **Collaborate with other defense/industrial accounts** — mutual thread tags, co-hosted Spaces
2. **Get controversial** (measured) — defense Twitter loves debate
3. **Start a weekly Space** — "Defense Industry Happy Hour" or "Ordnance & Operations"
4. **Pitch yourself for podcasts** — use Twitter threads as proof of expertise
5. **Leverage news cycles** — when defense news breaks, be the first operator to comment

### Month 6-12: Authority (10,000 → 25,000+ followers)

1. **Launch newsletter** (see monetization section)
2. **Get quoted/cited** by defense media — share threads with journalists
3. **Attend and live-tweet** from trade shows (SHOT Show, AUSA, DSEI, etc.)
4. **Build an email list** — convert followers to subscribers
5. **Start turning down low-value engagement** — curate your feed for quality

### Tier 2: Consulting & Deal Brokerage ($2K-$20K/mo) ⭐ PRIMARY TARGET
*Available from Day 1, scales with following*

This is where Brandon's background becomes a **money printer.**

#### Defense/Industrial Consulting
- **Offer:** "I help companies navigate defense industry entry, international arms regulations, and capital raises"
- **Rate:** $250-$500/hr or $5K-$15K/mo retainer
- **Lead generation:** Twitter threads demonstrate expertise → DMs from prospects → discovery calls
- **Target clients:** Defense startups, international buyers, investors exploring defense sector, companies needing ITAR/export guidance

#### Deal Brokerage
- **Offer:** Connect buyers and sellers of ammunition, ordnance, industrial equipment, heavy machinery
- **Revenue model:** 2-5% commission on deals brokered
- **Lead generation:** "Looking for [specific equipment/ordnance]? DM me." posts
- **This directly feeds into the Longer Industries deal brokerage platform vision**

#### How to generate consulting leads from Twitter:
1. Post expertise → builds credibility
2. End threads with "DM me if you're navigating this" or "I help companies with exactly this"
3. Use a Calendly link in bio for free 15-min discovery calls
4. Convert 15-min calls to paid engagements
5. As volume grows, raise prices and become more selective

### Tier 3: Newsletter & Info Products ($1K-$10K/mo)
*Launch at ~2,000 followers*

#### Newsletter: "The Ordnance Report" or "Longer Brief"
- **Platform:** Substack or Beehiiv (both have built-in monetization)
- **Free tier:** Weekly defense industry insights, deal flow commentary, fundraising lessons
- **Paid tier ($10-$25/mo):** Deep dives on specific markets, deal teardowns, regulatory guides, exclusive AMAs
- **Revenue math:**
  - 5,000 free subscribers → 2% paid conversion = 100 paid @ $15/mo = **$1,500/mo**
  - 15,000 free subscribers → 3% paid conversion = 450 paid @ $15/mo = **$6,750/mo**
- **Sponsorship revenue:** Defense companies, gun industry brands, industrial suppliers sponsor newsletters
  - At 5K subscribers: $200-$500/issue
  - At 15K subscribers: $500-$2,000/issue

#### Info Products (Launch 6-12 months in)
- **"How to Break Into the Defense Industry"** — digital guide/course ($97-$497)
  - Target: Entrepreneurs, veterans transitioning to defense business, investors

  - Target: Defense startup founders, first-time defense entrepreneurs
- **"International Arms Trade 101"** — navigating regulations, finding buyers, structuring deals ($297-$997)
  - Target: Companies looking to export, international trade professionals

#### Revenue from info products:
- Launch to 10K followers + 3K email list
- Conservative: 50-100 sales at $197 = **$10K-$20K per launch**
- These can be sold perpetually (evergreen)

## Key Principles

1. **Consistency > Virality** — Post every day for 90 days before evaluating. Most people quit at week 3.
2. **Expertise is the moat** — Anyone can have opinions. Few can say "I manufactured 10 million rounds."
3. **DMs are where money lives** — Public content builds authority; private conversations close deals.
4. **Don't sell on main** — 90% value, 10% ask. People will find your offers if the content is good.
5. **Speed on news** — When a defense story breaks, be the first operator to comment. This is how you go viral.
6. **Embrace controversy** — Measured hot takes drive 10x the engagement of safe posts. Don't be reckless, but don't be boring.
7. **Build the email list** — Twitter can change algorithms overnight. Your email list is yours forever.
8. **The compound effect is real** — Month 1 will feel like shouting into the void. Month 6 is where it starts clicking.
