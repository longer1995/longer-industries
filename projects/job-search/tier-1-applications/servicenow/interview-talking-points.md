# SERVICENOW - Interview Talking Points

## Opening Statement
"I've been both the founder implementing workflow automation to scale operations and the sales professional helping enterprises optimize their processes. ServiceNow sits at the intersection of both experiences—that's why I'm here."

## Core Value Propositions

### 1. Real Workflow Automation Experience
- **Story:** "At American Munitions, I built custom workflows for international compliance—tracking materials from Pakistan manufacturers through customs to our Texas assembly facility. We processed hundreds of SKUs with zero compliance violations."
- **Benefit:** "When prospects ask about workflow automation ROI, I can share actual metrics, not just promises."

### 2. High-Growth Company Understanding
- **Story:** "I took a company from zero to $1.8M revenue in 3 years while managing international expansion. I know what it's like when your processes can't keep up with your growth."
- **Benefit:** "ServiceNow's high-growth clients are experiencing exactly what I've lived through—I can speak their language."

### 3. Enterprise IT Relationships
- **Story:** "In my valve industry role, I worked directly with IT procurement teams at chemical plants and refineries. These are 24/7 operations where system downtime costs millions per hour."
- **Benefit:** "I understand enterprise IT decision-making, compliance requirements, and the critical nature of reliability."

### 4. Founder's Sales Instincts
- **Story:** "When I pitched investors for $3M in funding, I learned to identify pain points, quantify ROI, and structure deals that work for all parties."
- **Benefit:** "I don't just present solutions—I understand how to structure deals that get approved by CFOs and procurement teams."

## Objection Handling

### "You don't have SaaS experience"
**Response:** "True, but I have something better—I've been the customer. I've evaluated enterprise software as a CEO managing P&L pressure. I know exactly what your prospects are thinking during demos because I've been in their seat. That's more valuable than just knowing how to pitch software."

### "ServiceNow is very technical—can you handle IT conversations?"
**Response:** "I managed manufacturing systems, quality control databases, and international logistics platforms. While I'm not writing code, I understand system integrations, data flows, and IT architecture well enough to have meaningful conversations with CTOs and IT directors."

### "Why leave entrepreneurship for employee role?"
**Response:** "I love building, and ServiceNow gives me the tools to build at scale. Instead of optimizing one company's workflows, I can help dozens of enterprises transform their operations. Plus, ServiceNow's growth trajectory is exactly what I experienced as a founder—exciting but with more resources."

### "Can you handle rejection in sales?"
**Response:** "I've had investors say no to funding requests, international partners cancel major deals, and customers reject proposals after months of work. Sales rejection is nothing compared to watching your company nearly fail due to a business partner's embezzlement. I bounce back fast."
