# SERVICENOW - Application Strategy & Timeline

## Application Submission Strategy

### 1. Role Prioritization
**PRIMARY TARGET:** Account Executive (if 2+ years experience requirement is flexible)
**SECONDARY:** Sales Development Representative (guaranteed entry point)
**STRATEGY:** Apply to both simultaneously—shows ambition but willingness to grow

### 2. Application Timing
- **Best Days:** Tuesday-Wednesday, 10 AM-2 PM CST
- **Avoid:** End of quarter (ServiceNow runs on quarterly cycles)
- **Target:** Submit early in the week for maximum recruiter attention

### 3. LinkedIn Optimization
**IMMEDIATE UPDATES:**
- [ ] Headline: "Operations & Sales Leader | Founded & Scaled Manufacturing Operations | Seeking High-Growth Sales Role"
- [ ] Skills: Add "ServiceNow," "Workflow Automation," "Enterprise Sales," "Process Optimization"
- [ ] Post content about digital transformation in manufacturing
- [ ] Connect with ServiceNow employees, especially in Texas

### 4. Application Channels (Priority Order)

#### **PRIMARY: ServiceNow Careers Portal**
- careers.servicenow.com
- Upload targeted resume highlighting operational/process experience
- Use cover letter emphasizing high-growth company fit
- Apply to Houston-area roles first, then Dallas/Austin

#### **SECONDARY: Referral Strategy**  
- Target ServiceNow Account Executives, SDRs, Sales Engineers in Texas
- Focus on people with manufacturing/energy backgrounds
- Offer value: insights about enterprise software buying decisions

#### **TERTIARY: Recruiter Network**
- ServiceNow uses external recruiters heavily
- Search LinkedIn: "ServiceNow recruiter," "Enterprise SaaS recruiter"
- Target recruiters with recent ServiceNow placements

## Key Messaging by Stakeholder

### **Hiring Managers:**
- Focus on results and scalability
- Emphasize founder credibility with enterprise buyers
- Highlight Houston market knowledge (energy/manufacturing)

### **ServiceNow Employees:**
- Ask about culture and growth opportunities
- Share genuine interest in workflow automation
- Offer insights about enterprise software buying process

### **Customers/Partners:**
- Learn about their ServiceNow implementation experience
- Understand common challenges and success factors
- Build credibility through industry knowledge

## Red Flags to Avoid
- Don't oversell technical knowledge (stay business-focused)
- Don't appear to be fleeing failed companies (frame as growth opportunity)
- Don't suggest ServiceNow competes with your previous experience (complement, not compete)
- Don't rush the process (ServiceNow values cultural fit)

## Interview Preparation Priorities

### **For AE Role:**

2. Prepare complex deal scenarios from founder experience
