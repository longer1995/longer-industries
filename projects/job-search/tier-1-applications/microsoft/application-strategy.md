# MICROSOFT - Application Strategy & Timeline

## Application Submission Strategy

### 1. Role Targeting Priority
**PRIMARY:** Enterprise Sales Specialist - Manufacturing/Industrial
**SECONDARY:** Account Executive - Commercial/Enterprise  
**TERTIARY:** Business Development - Azure Industrial IoT
**STRATEGY:** Focus on roles emphasizing industry expertise over pure cloud technical knowledge

### 2. Application Timing
- **Best Days:** Tuesday-Thursday, 9 AM-12 PM CST  
- **Avoid:** Month/quarter end (Microsoft has strong quarterly rhythm)
- **Target:** Early week submission for recruiter visibility
- **Seasonal:** Avoid major Azure conferences (Build, Ignite) when hiring teams are distracted

### 3. LinkedIn Profile Optimization

- [ ] Headline: "Manufacturing & International Business Leader | $3M+ Raised | Seeking Enterprise Sales Role at Microsoft"

- careers.microsoft.com

## Key Messaging by Stakeholder

### **Hiring Managers:**
- Emphasize ROI and results orientation
- Highlight founder credibility with C-level executives  
- Focus on Houston market opportunity (energy/manufacturing hub)

### **Microsoft Sales Team:**
- Share genuine excitement about Microsoft's platform approach
- Ask about collaboration between sales specialists and solution architects
- Offer insights about enterprise software evaluation process

### **Technical Staff (Solution Architects):**
- Acknowledge technical complexity without claiming to be architect
- Share real-world implementation challenges from founder experience
- Ask about customer technical objections and how sales/technical teams collaborate

## Interview Preparation Strategy

### **Technical Readiness (Business Level)**
1. **Azure Fundamentals:** Understand core services, not implementation details
2. **Dynamics 365:** Focus on Supply Chain Management and Manufacturing modules
3. **Power Platform:** Business process automation and custom applications
4. **Mixed Reality:** HoloLens industrial use cases and ROI stories

### **Industry Knowledge Deep Dive**  
1. **Manufacturing Challenges:** Digital transformation barriers, legacy system integration
2. **Energy Sector:** Operational technology (OT) vs information technology (IT) convergence
3. **International Business:** Compliance frameworks, data sovereignty requirements
4. **Supply Chain:** Visibility, optimization, risk management

### **Microsoft Culture Preparation**
1. **Growth Mindset:** Continuous learning, embracing challenges
2. **Customer Obsession:** Understanding customer outcomes, not just technology features
3. **Diversity & Inclusion:** Inclusive collaboration, diverse perspectives
4. **Partner Success:** Ecosystem approach, win-win relationships

## Red Flags to Avoid
- Don't claim deep technical expertise (stay business-focused)
- Don't oversell manufacturing experience as only relevant background
- Don't appear to be running from failed ventures (frame as growth opportunity)  
- Don't criticize AWS or Google (acknowledge competition professionally)
- Don't rush the process (Microsoft values cultural fit and thorough evaluation)
