## Objection Handling

### **"You don't have Microsoft/cloud experience"**
**Response:** "You're right, I haven't sold Microsoft solutions—but I've been the customer. I've evaluated cloud platforms as a CEO managing P&L pressure. I know exactly what your prospects are thinking during ROI discussions because I've sat in their chair."

### **"Manufacturing is very technical—can you handle complex solutions?"**  
**Response:** "I managed manufacturing systems for precision ammunition—tolerance measurements in thousandths of inches, regulatory compliance for international shipping, quality systems for defense contracts. I'm technical enough to be credible, business-focused enough to sell outcomes."

### **"Why leave entrepreneurship for corporate sales?"**
**Response:** "I love building, and Microsoft gives me the resources to build at enterprise scale. Instead of transforming one manufacturing company, I can help dozens modernize their operations. Plus, Microsoft's platform approach means I'm solving systematic problems, not just point solutions."

### **"Can you work within Microsoft's sales process?"**
**Response:** "I've managed complex B2B sales cycles with multiple stakeholders, regulatory approvals, and international logistics. Microsoft's enterprise sales process is sophisticated, but I've navigated more complex deal structures with less support."
