## Interview Questions They Might Ask

### **"Why Microsoft over AWS or Google?"**
"Microsoft is the only cloud provider that understands enterprise manufacturing. AWS is infrastructure-first, Google is AI-first, but Microsoft is business-solution-first. When a plant manager needs to integrate IoT data with ERP systems and visualize it in familiar Office tools, only Microsoft delivers that seamlessly."

### **"How do you handle highly technical sales conversations?"**
"I've managed technical manufacturing processes but I'm not trying to be the solution architect. My role is translating business challenges into Microsoft solutions and connecting technical capabilities to ROI. I know enough to be credible, not enough to get lost in technical details."

### **"What's your approach to long enterprise sales cycles?"**
"I raised $3M over 18 months, managing multiple investor relationships with different decision timelines. Enterprise sales is similar—multiple stakeholders, complex evaluation processes, consensus-building requirements. I'm comfortable with 12-18 month cycles because I understand how enterprise decisions get made."

### **"How do you stay current with rapidly evolving cloud technologies?"**
"As a founder, I had to constantly evaluate new technologies for potential competitive advantage. I'm comfortable being a continuous learner. Microsoft's ecosystem is vast, but I focus on deep expertise in manufacturing/industrial solutions rather than trying to be expert in everything."
