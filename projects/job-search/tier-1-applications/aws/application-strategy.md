# AWS - Application Strategy & Timeline

## Application Submission Strategy

### 1. Role Targeting Priority
**PRIMARY:** Account Executive - Enterprise (Manufacturing/Industrial focus)
**SECONDARY:** Account Executive - Commercial (Mid-market manufacturing)
**TERTIARY:** Business Development Representative (entry point with growth potential)
**STRATEGY:** Focus on roles emphasizing industry expertise and enterprise sales over pure technical cloud knowledge

### 2. Application Timing
- **Best Days:** Monday-Wednesday, 9 AM-1 PM CST
- **Avoid:** AWS re:Invent conference periods, end of AWS fiscal year (December)
- **Target:** Early week for recruiter visibility, avoid major tech event seasons
- **Seasonal:** Best months are January-March and August-October for hiring

### 3. LinkedIn Profile Optimization
**IMMEDIATE UPDATES:**
- [ ] Headline: "Manufacturing & International Operations Leader | Founded & Scaled Tech-Enabled Manufacturing | Seeking AWS Enterprise Sales Role"
- [ ] Skills: Add "AWS," "Cloud Computing," "Manufacturing," "IoT," "International Business," "Enterprise Sales"
- [ ] Summary: Lead with manufacturing systems + cloud transformation angle
- [ ] Activity: Post about industrial cloud adoption, manufacturing digitalization trends

### 4. Application Channels (Priority Order)

#### **PRIMARY: AWS Careers Portal**
- amazon.jobs (AWS roles)
- Create detailed profile highlighting technical manufacturing background
- Upload resume emphasizing scaling operations + technical implementation
- Use cover letter focusing on industrial cloud transformation experience

#### **SECONDARY: Internal Referral Strategy**
- Target AWS employees with manufacturing/industrial backgrounds
- Focus on Enterprise Sales team in Texas region
- Connect with AWS solution architects who serve manufacturing clients
- Leverage LinkedIn to find shared connections with AWS employees

#### **TERTIARY: AWS Partner Network**
- Connect with AWS consulting partners serving manufacturing (Deloitte, Accenture, etc.)
- Research AWS Advanced Partners with manufacturing specializations
- Attend AWS partner events or manufacturing industry conferences

### 5. Technical Preparation
**AWS Fundamentals to Master:**
- AWS Well-Architected Framework basics
- IoT Core, SiteWise, and manufacturing-specific services
- Basic understanding of EC2, RDS, S3, and core services
- Industry solutions and customer case studies

## Key Messaging by Stakeholder

### **Hiring Managers:**
- Emphasize unique combination of technical + business + sales experience
- Highlight proven ability to build enterprise relationships
- Focus on Houston market opportunity and existing network

### **AWS Sales Team:**
- Share genuine excitement about AWS's technical capabilities
- Ask about collaboration between sales and solution architecture
- Offer insights about enterprise technology decision-making process

### **Technical Staff (Solution Architects):**
- Acknowledge technical complexity without claiming to be architect
- Share real-world implementation challenges from manufacturing experience
- Ask about common customer technical objections and solutions

### **AWS Partners:**
- Discuss collaboration opportunities with manufacturing expertise
- Share industry knowledge about customer challenges and requirements
- Explore potential joint customer engagement opportunities

## Interview Preparation Strategy

### **Technical Readiness (Business Level)**
1. **AWS Core Services:** Understand EC2, RDS, S3, VPC basics
2. **Manufacturing Solutions:** Deep dive on IoT Core, SiteWise, analytics services
3. **Customer Case Studies:** Research AWS manufacturing customer wins
4. **Competitive Landscape:** Understand positioning vs. Microsoft, Google

### **Industry Knowledge Deep Dive**
1. **Manufacturing 4.0:** Digital transformation trends, Industry 4.0 concepts
2. **Industrial IoT:** Equipment monitoring, predictive maintenance, quality control
3. **Supply Chain:** Visibility, optimization, risk management, international complexity
4. **Regulatory Compliance:** Manufacturing quality standards, international trade

### **AWS Culture & Leadership Principles**
1. **Customer Obsession:** Focus on customer outcomes over technology features
2. **Ownership:** Take responsibility for entire customer success
3. **Think Big:** Help customers transform entire operations, not just solve point problems
4. **Learn and Be Curious:** Continuous learning about AWS services and customer needs

## Red Flags to Avoid
- Don't claim to be a cloud architect (stay business-focused)
- Don't oversell manufacturing as only relevant experience
- Don't appear to be running from failed companies (frame as growth opportunity)
- Don't minimize AWS's technical complexity (acknowledge learning curve)
- Don't rush the technical learning (show commitment to mastering AWS solutions)

## Negotiation Strategy
**When offer comes:**
1. **Express enthusiasm** for AWS and the role first
2. **Reference market research** on AWS enterprise sales compensation
3. **Highlight unique value** of manufacturing expertise in AWS ecosystem
4. **Request 24-48 hours** to review and respond professionally
5. **Negotiate total compensation** including base, variable, equity, benefits

- Manufacturing expertise commands premium in cloud sales
- Founder experience suggests faster ramp time and higher deal values
- Houston market relationships provide immediate competitive advantage
- International business experience valuable for AWS global enterprise accounts
