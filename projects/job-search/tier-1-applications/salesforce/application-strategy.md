# SALESFORCE - Application Strategy & Timeline

## Application Submission Strategy

### 1. Application Timing
- **Best Days:** Tuesday-Thursday, 9-11 AM CST
- **Avoid:** Monday mornings, Friday afternoons, end of quarter (March 31)
- **Target:** Submit by Wednesday morning for maximum visibility

### 2. LinkedIn Preparation
**IMMEDIATE ACTIONS:**
- [ ] Update LinkedIn headline: "Enterprise Sales Leader | $3M+ Raised | Manufacturing & Defense Expert | Seeking Enterprise AE Role"
- [ ] Add Salesforce skills: "Enterprise Software Sales," "Manufacturing Cloud," "CRM Strategy"
- [ ] Post thought leadership content about manufacturing digitalization
- [ ] Connect with Salesforce employees in Houston area

### 3. Application Channels (Priority Order)

#### **PRIMARY: Direct Application**
- Apply through careers.salesforce.com
- Use cover letter exactly as written
- Upload clean resume highlighting founder experience
- Apply to Houston-based Enterprise AE roles first

#### **SECONDARY: Referral Network**
- Target connections: Salesforce AEs, Sales Engineers, Sales Managers in Houston
- Message template: "I'm a former founder with manufacturing expertise interested in Salesforce's Manufacturing Cloud. Would love 10 minutes of your insight."
- Offer value: "I can share what I learned about enterprise software buying decisions as a former CEO"

#### **TERTIARY: Recruiter Network**
- Identify Salesforce-focused recruiters on LinkedIn
- Search terms: "Salesforce recruiter," "Enterprise sales recruiter Houston"
- Approach after direct application to show genuine interest

### 4. Resume Optimization for ATS
**Keywords to Include:**
- Enterprise sales, B2B sales, manufacturing, industrial, CRM
- Salesforce, SaaS, software sales, enterprise accounts
- Fundraising, capital raising, international business
- Houston, Texas, energy sector, oil and gas
- Account management, relationship building, quota attainment

## Follow-Up Timeline

### **Week 1: Application Submission**
**Day 1 (Monday):** Submit application via careers.salesforce.com
**Day 3 (Wednesday):** LinkedIn connection requests to 3-5 Salesforce Houston employees
**Day 5 (Friday):** Post LinkedIn content about manufacturing digitalization trends

### **Week 2: Initial Outreach**
**Day 8 (Monday):** Follow up with LinkedIn connections who accepted
**Day 10 (Wednesday):** Send personalized message to Houston-based Salesforce hiring manager (if identifiable)
**Day 12 (Friday):** Apply to 2-3 additional Enterprise AE roles at Salesforce

### **Week 3: Expand Network**
**Day 15 (Monday):** Connect with Salesforce customers in Houston manufacturing/energy
**Day 17 (Wednesday):** Attend Houston Sales/Tech meetups (research events)
**Day 19 (Friday):** Share case study about international business development

### **Week 4: Direct Outreach**
**Day 22 (Monday):** Email hiring manager directly (if contact found)
**Day 24 (Wednesday):** Follow up on application status
**Day 26 (Friday):** Consider reaching out to recruiter

Would love to share insights and learn more about Salesforce's approach to the manufacturing vertical."

[Phone] | longerindustrial@gmail.com"

## Red Flags to Avoid
- Don't appear desperate (space out applications/follow-ups)
- Don't oversell the "no degree" angle (mention once, move on)
- Don't trash-talk previous companies/partners
- Don't focus too much on defense industry (could be polarizing)
- Don't seem like you're just trying to get any sales job

## Success Metrics
- **Week 1:** Application submitted, 3+ LinkedIn connections
- **Week 2:** 1+ meaningful conversation with Salesforce employee
- **Week 3:** Hiring manager contact identified and approached
- **Week 4:** Phone screen scheduled or clear next steps identified

## Backup Plan
If no response after 4 weeks:
1. Apply to Salesforce roles in Dallas, Austin (Texas expansion)
2. Consider Sales Development Rep role as entry point
3. Target Salesforce partners/implementation companies
4. Leverage network for warm introduction to Salesforce leadership
