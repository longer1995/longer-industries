# SALESFORCE - Interview Talking Points

## Opening Statement
"I've been on both sides of enterprise sales—as a founder buying technology to scale operations, and as a sales leader building relationships with Fortune 500 clients. This dual perspective is what I bring to Salesforce."

## Core Value Propositions

### 1. Authentic Enterprise Credibility
- **Story:** "When I founded American Munitions, I had to choose between three different ERP systems for a $1.8M/year operation. I know what it's like to be pitched by 10 different vendors in a month."
- **Benefit:** "Your enterprise clients will recognize immediately that I understand their challenges because I've lived them."

### 2. Crisis Management & Relationship Preservation
- **Story:** "When my business partner embezzled $1M, I had to maintain relationships with international clients while restructuring the company. We kept every major account."
- **Benefit:** "Enterprise accounts face crises. I know how to be a trusted advisor, not just a vendor, during difficult times."

### 3. International Complexity
- **Story:** "I've negotiated arms deals across Eastern Europe and managed supply chains from Pakistan to South America. Different currencies, regulations, cultures."
- **Benefit:** "Salesforce's enterprise clients are global. I understand the complexity of international business operations."

### 4. Manufacturing & Industrial Expertise
- **Story:** "At the valve company, I sold severe service solutions to chemical plants and refineries—mission-critical applications where downtime costs millions per hour."
- **Benefit:** "I can speak the language of manufacturing, energy, and industrial clients—major Salesforce verticals."

## Objection Handling

### "You don't have SaaS experience"
**Response:** "You're right, I haven't sold software as a service. But I have sold business transformation as a result. My clients didn't buy valves—they bought uptime. They didn't buy ammunition—they bought security. Salesforce clients aren't buying CRM—they're buying revenue growth. That's what I know how to sell."

### "No college degree?"
**Response:** "When I was a sophomore, I had to choose between finishing college or joining a startup that was raising $12M. I chose results over credentials. Six years later, I've raised $3M myself, built international operations, and managed P&Ls. Salesforce values impact—my track record is my degree."

### "Why sales instead of founding another company?"
**Response:** "I'm a builder, and right now I want to build within a platform that's already changing the world. Salesforce gives me the resources, brand credibility, and scale I could never achieve alone. Plus, I have two young kids—I want the excitement of enterprise deals with more predictable income."

## Questions to Ask Them

### About the Role
- "What's the typical deal size and sales cycle for enterprise accounts in Houston?"
- "How does Salesforce support AEs who are building relationships in traditional industries like energy and manufacturing?"
- "What's the mix between new logos and account expansion in this territory?"

### About Success
- "What separates your top-performing enterprise AEs from the average ones?"
- "How do you measure success beyond quota—customer retention, expansion, satisfaction?"
- "What's the career trajectory for someone who excels in this role?"

### About Culture
- "How does Salesforce support AEs who bring non-traditional backgrounds but proven results?"
- "What's the collaboration like between AEs and solution engineers for complex enterprise deals?"
