# PRICING STRATEGY - Premium Positioning for Immediate Revenue

**Core Philosophy:** Position as premium expert, not commodity consultant. Price for value delivered, not time spent.

## Rate Structure

### Crisis Consultation Package
**$5,000 flat rate / 48-hour delivery**
- Premium pricing justified by immediate availability
- Flat rate eliminates negotiation friction  
- Higher profit margin than hourly work
- Attracts serious buyers with budget authority
- Target: 3-5 packages per month = $15K-$25K

### Standard Hourly Consulting
**$500-750/hour depending on scope and urgency**

**$750/hour:** Crisis/emergency work requiring immediate response
- Equipment failures requiring overnight solutions
- Compliance emergencies with tight deadlines
- Time-sensitive international negotiations
- Mission-critical manufacturing problems

**$600/hour:** Specialized expertise in high-value situations  
- International defense trade consulting
- Complex manufacturing optimization
- High-stakes business development
- Technical problem-solving in severe service applications

**$500/hour:** Standard consulting and advisory work
- Regular business development support
- Ongoing manufacturing advice
- Process optimization projects
- Strategic planning and analysis

### Project-Based Consulting
**Custom pricing based on scope and value**
- Business development retainers: $10K-25K/month
- Manufacturing optimization projects: $15K-50K total
- International market entry: $20K-75K depending on complexity
- M&A due diligence: $25K-100K depending on deal size

## Competitive Positioning

**Advantage:** Speed, expertise level, no bureaucracy

**Them:** $200-400/hour, narrow specialization, academic approach

**Them:** $100-300/hour, project-based, limited availability

**Advantage:** Premium positioning, immediate availability, results focus

## Pricing Psychology

### Premium Positioning Benefits
- **Quality Signal:** High rates indicate high value
- **Buyer Qualification:** Attracts serious clients with real budgets  
- **Authority Building:** Positions you as top-tier expert
- **Profit Maximization:** Better margins than volume-based approaches

### Rate Anchoring Strategy
**Lead with Crisis Package:** $5K establishes premium position
**Position Hourly as Alternative:** "Or $750/hour for ongoing work"
**Emphasize Value:** Focus on outcomes, not inputs

### Objection Handling

**"Your rates are higher than others"**
*"You're right - because this isn't commodity consulting. You're getting 15 years of experience in defense manufacturing, international trade, and crisis response. When you need immediate expert solutions, premium pricing reflects premium value."*

**"Can you work for less?"**  
*"I could, but then I'd need to take on multiple clients to maintain quality of life. That means you wouldn't get the immediate availability and focused attention that makes this valuable. The premium rate ensures you get premium service."*

**"We usually pay $300/hour"**
*"That's fair for general consulting. But when your production line is down, compliance deadline is tomorrow, or $2M deal is at risk, you need someone who's solved exactly these problems before. Emergency medicine costs more than routine checkups for the same reason."*

## Implementation Tactics

### Rate Communication Strategy
**Never apologize for pricing.** Present rates confidently as value propositions.

**Package rates together:**
- "Crisis consultation: $5K for 48-hour delivery"
- "Ongoing consulting: $500-750/hour depending on scope"
- "Project work: Custom pricing based on value delivered"

**Emphasize scarcity:**
- "I only take 2-3 crisis consultations per month to ensure quality"
- "Currently booking 2 weeks out for standard consulting"
- "Limited availability for new project work"

### Payment Terms
**Crisis Consultation:** 100% upfront before work begins
**Hourly Work:** 50% retainer, weekly invoicing thereafter  
**Projects:** 50% upfront, milestones or monthly thereafter

### Rate Testing Strategy
**Week 1:** Start with standard rates ($500-750/hour, $5K crisis)
**Week 2-4:** Monitor market response and conversion rates
**Month 2:** Adjust rates based on demand (raise if overbooked, maintain if balanced)

## Rate Adjustment Triggers

### Raise Rates When:
- Booked 3+ weeks out consistently
- Converting 80%+ of qualified prospects
- Getting multiple crisis requests per week
- Clients accepting rates without negotiation

### Maintain Rates When:  
- Converting 50-70% of qualified prospects
- Booking 1-2 weeks out consistently
- Getting regular inquiries and RFPs
- Clients negotiating but ultimately accepting

### Strategy Adjustment When:
- Converting <30% of qualified prospects
- Getting rate objections consistently  
- Struggling to book minimum hours needed
- Losing deals primarily on price

**Bottom Line: Start premium, prove value, raise rates as demand increases.**
