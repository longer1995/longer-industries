# Longer CRM — Strategy & Edge

## The one-line thesis
Every sales-conversation tool today is sold to the **boss, to watch the rep**.
We build the inverse: **rep-first voice capture** where the rep owns their book of
business and shares only the deal points the company needs. CRM-agnostic.
The entry layer for voice into every CRM.

## Competitive landscape (the gap)
| Category | Players | Gap they leave |
|---|---|---|
| Meeting notetakers | Otter, Fireflies, Granola, Fathom | Meeting-first, not phone/field, not deal-structured, employer-owned |
| Conversation intelligence | Gong, Chorus/ZoomInfo, Salesloft | Manager-surveillance, enterprise-only, expensive, reps resent them |
| Field sales | Siro | Manager coaching tool, single-purpose, not CRM-agnostic |
| Hardware | Plaud, Limitless | No deal structure, no CRM pipeline |
| CRM-native voice | Zoho Voice, Salesforce Einstein | Clunky, locked to one CRM |

**Nobody owns: CRM-agnostic + rep-first + phone/field + deal-structured + rep
controls what the employer sees.**

## The moat (it is NOT the tech)
Transcription (Whisper/Deepgram) and LLM extraction (Claude) are commodities.
The defensibility is:
1. **Positioning** — rep-first. Gong *can't* copy it without cannibalizing its
   boss-paid business.
2. **Relationship graph** — compounds per user, creates switching cost (data network effect).
3. **UX** — so fast reps choose it over the company-mandated tool.

## Go-to-market (how you reach 250k)
Bottom-up PLG (Superhuman / Notion / Granola / Claude Code path):
reps bring it themselves → spreads desk to desk → teams standardize →
THEN sell the enterprise control plane (transparency tier w/ rep-consent) to management.
The enterprise/Salesforce acquisition thesis = own the voice layer across all CRMs.

Wedge audiences:
- Owner-led SMBs with no CRM or a bad one (they have nothing to lose).
- Individual reps at bigger companies who want their book protected.

Marketing hook that spreads on LinkedIn/sales Twitter:
**"Your boss can't see your relationships. Only your deals."**

## Killer features (ranked by edge)
1. **Book-of-Business Vault** — only deal points/numbers sync to employer CRM;
   relationships/contacts/notes stay in a rep-private vault that travels between jobs.
2. **One-tap "off the record"** — wall off a call segment mid-call; never extracted.
3. **Relationship Memory** — per-contact graph auto-built from calls (family names,
   birthdays, interests, prior deals, deal values, history).
4. **Seamless action commands** — "Jason invited you to happy hour Jul 1 7pm —
   add to calendar?" One tap → calendar / reminder / draft quote / log deal.
5. **CRM-agnostic** — one capture layer, any CRM. "Voice assist for every CRM."

## Build order (MVP wedge — don't boil the ocean)
1. Capture (mic + upload) → structured deal card  ✅ backend built
2. **Book-of-Business vault** (rep-private vs employer-synced split)  ← the wedge
3. **Relationship memory** per contact
4. **One** action command end-to-end (calendar invite)
5. HubSpot/Zoho push  ✅ adapters built
