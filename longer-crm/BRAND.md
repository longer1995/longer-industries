# Brand & affiliation

**Longer CRM** is a standalone product. It carries its own name and brand.

**Attribution:** "by Atlas Agentics" — a maker credit, shown as muted secondary
text beneath the product name (login screen, app home, footer/about). It is *not*
co-branding: Atlas Agentics lends credibility as the maker; Longer CRM stands on
its own.

## Usage rules
- Product name is always **Longer CRM** (short form: "Longer"). Never "Atlas CRM".
- Attribution line is always lowercase-styled **"by Atlas Agentics"**, smaller and
  dimmer than the product name. Never larger or equal weight.
- Do not imply Atlas Agentics operates, supports, or is liable for the product in
  legal/ToS copy. The relationship displayed to users is maker attribution only.

## Where it appears
| Surface | Placement |
|---|---|
| PWA login/auth | under the "Longer CRM" title |
| PWA footer | quiet "by Atlas Agentics" line |
| Native home | under the title (`maker` style in HomeScreen.tsx) |
| App stores / manifest | name "Longer CRM", publisher Atlas Agentics |
